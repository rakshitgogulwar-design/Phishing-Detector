About PhishGuard Phishing Detection Platform

Designed for cybersecurity learners, academic research, and real-time threat protection.

Detection Methodology

PhishGuard employs a defense-in-depth hybrid architecture uniting three distinct analytical pillars:

  1. Passive Rule-Based Analysis: Inspects URL lexical syntax, character entropy, numeric ratios, non-standard ports, and query tokens without exposing browsers to hostile payloads.
  2. Calibrated Machine Learning: Employs gradient-boosted decision trees trained on multi-modal features with strict domain-grouped stratification to prevent zero-day data leakage.
  3. Active Threat Intelligence: Dynamically correlates targets against 30+ brand dictionaries and 40+ high-abuse TLD registries to uncover deceptive brand spoofing.

Standardized Scoring Schema

0 – 30 = SAFE Authentic domain identity, verified TLS encryption, and standard protocol properties.
31 – 60 = SUSPICIOUS Elevated anomalies such as URL shorteners, multiple subdomains, or unranked newly registered TLDs.
61 – 100 = HIGH RISK / PHISHING Critical threat indicators detected including brand spoofing, raw IP addresses, credential traps, or homoglyphs.
